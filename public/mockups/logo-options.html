Stelnyx logo — 4 directions

Four candidate marks, rendered at favicon (16px), nav (32px), and hero (96px) scale, on dark and light. Wordmark lockups paired with each. Picks should survive being shrunk to a Safari tab favicon — that is the real test.

Recommended: Option E — A's mark + B's wordmark (spaced caps), with tracking dialed to 0.18em, weight to 400, and a compact "Stelnyx" alt for small surfaces. Hybrid lockup system: spaced for hero/marketing, compact for nav/footer/tabs. Lowest risk while preserving brand recognition.
